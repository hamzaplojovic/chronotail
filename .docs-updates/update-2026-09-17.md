C-ABI-v2 redesign. README, architecture, durability, migration, API guides,
benchmark evidence, contributor/security guidance, release notes, design
records, and performance reports were checked against the implementation.

## Current evidence

The current public run reports 28.14M records/s for 4,096-point batch append,
1.64M queries/s for warm random lookup, 1.22M queries/s for a warm raw
100-point range, 355.31k queries/s for a 100-point raw aggregate, and 7.40M
reader queries/s with 32 readers while writing. Full-series summary calls are
presented as bounded-path evidence rather than a hardware-throughput headline
because their measured intervals approach timer and loop overhead.

The clean internal profile remains separate from competitive marketing. Its
saved before/after runs, allocation counters, simulator evidence, rejected
experiments, and predicted follow-up targets are documented in the two
performance reports.

## Validation

- Published artifacts have exact expected row/group counts and identities.
- Every published artifact passes its `SHA256SUMS` manifest.
- Current and historical public runs are independently checked by
  `scripts/check-release.py`.
- SVG assets are XML-parseable, and no benchmark or architecture PNG mirror is
  retained.
- Documentation links, API examples, release metadata, all Zig test modes, and
  formatting are release gates rather than prose-only claims.

## Maintenance guidance

Keep historical reports immutable, preserve rejected performance experiments,
and publish a benchmark only when raw evidence, environment, commands, matrix,
generated report, SVGs, and checksums agree. Predictions in the optimization
roadmap are experiment budgets, not promises; replace them with measured
evidence after each accepted or rejected iteration.
